Depth: lite · Data: LOCAL DOCS + LIVE SEARCH 2026-11-17 (corrected same day: prices are the per-region files, not top-level pricing.json)

**Promise now:** human-written, ATS-friendly CVs fitted to the visitor's GCC market (or India → GCC), with LinkedIn, coaching and jobs in one place; prices in local currency; free CV review to start.

**Proof shown on site:** "500+ professionals supported" (verify this is a real count; PRODUCT.md bans unverified numbers), "Human written & reviewed", live Google reviews section.

**What customers praise most:** unknown (reviews not read).

**Gap:** every GCC competitor claims "region fit". Price is the sharpest real difference, and it runs the other way from what buyers expect: a human-written Kuwait CV from 4 KWD (Essential) to 10 KWD (Executive), where Labeeb advertises from KWD 25 and full packages at KWD 54-146 (labeeb.ae, read via search 2026-11-17). The site doesn't use this as an angle, and AI answers on "how much does a CV writer cost in Kuwait" quote only Labeeb's range.

**★ Angle to test:** "A human-written, ATS-ready Kuwait CV for 4 KWD, with a free review before you pay." Lead with the price as a citable fact, and handle the obvious objection ("is it cheap because it's a template?") with how the writing actually works.
