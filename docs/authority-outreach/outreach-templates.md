# Outreach Email Templates (Unit 8)

Every template below is a **starting point to personalize before sending** - none of these are
ever auto-sent, and none should go out unedited. Never use "Dear webmaster" or a false "I've been
following your blog for years" opener. See [README.md](./README.md) for the broader outreach
policy these templates operate under.

## A. Resource-page suggestion

> Subject: A resource for your [topic] page
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I came across your page on [specific page/topic] and found [specific genuine detail about it].
> I wanted to flag a free checklist we put together for GCC job seekers -
> brandmyprofile.com/resources/gcc-cv-checklist - in case it's useful to your readers alongside
> what you already have.
>
> No pressure either way, and thanks for putting the resource together.
>
> [Name], BrandMyProfile

## B. Broken/outdated-resource replacement

> Subject: A dead link on [page name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I was reading your page on [topic] and noticed the link to [old resource] no longer works
> ([describe what happens - 404, redirect, etc.]). If it's helpful, we have a current guide on
> [topic] at [specific URL] that covers similar ground.
>
> Either way, just wanted to flag the broken link.
>
> [Name], BrandMyProfile

## C. Expert contribution

> Subject: Contribution for [publication/site name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I work on CV writing and career positioning for GCC and India job seekers at BrandMyProfile, and
> noticed [publication] covers [related topic]. If useful, I'd be glad to contribute a short,
> practical piece or a few quotes on [specific narrow topic, e.g. "how ATS systems actually parse
> CVs"] - happy to keep it strictly to what we actually work on day to day.
>
> Let me know if that's of interest.
>
> [Name], BrandMyProfile

## D. Guest-expert-article pitch

> Subject: Guest article idea: [specific topic]

>

>
> [Name], BrandMyProfile

## E. Community-resource submission

> Subject: Resource suggestion for [community/group name]
>
> Hi [Name / mods],
>
> I help job seekers with CV writing for GCC and India markets. I put together a free GCC CV
> checklist (brandmyprofile.com/resources/gcc-cv-checklist) that might be a useful pinned resource
> or FAQ link for [community name], if that fits your rules on resource-sharing.
>
> Thanks for running the community - let me know if it's not a fit.
>
> [Name], BrandMyProfile

## Guest-content rules (applies to C, D and any accepted pitch)

- Only pitch genuinely relevant hosts whose audience actually cares about this content.
- Content must be genuinely useful on its own merits, not thin filler built around a link.
- Attribution must be truthful - no fabricated author bio or credentials.
- Links included must be editorial and contextual, never disguised paid placements.
- Avoid mass guest-post marketplaces, PBNs, link farms, and "write for us, pay for a dofollow
  link" sites entirely - these are exactly the manufactured-backlink pattern this unit exists to
  avoid.
