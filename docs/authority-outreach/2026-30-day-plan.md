### The 4 primary assets
1. **GCC CV Checklist** - angle: a free checklist to review a CV before a Gulf application. Why link: practical, printable, no sign-up. Referrers: GCC career blogs, communities, training institutes, coaches.
2. **Kuwait CV Format Guide** - angle: what to include and leave off a CV for Kuwait. Strongest section: Personal Details + Photo guidance. Referrers: Kuwait expat resources, Kuwait career pages.
3. **ATS CV Guide Kuwait** - angle: what ATS software really does with a CV. Strongest section: Common Formatting Problems. Referrers: HR blogs, recruitment education, university career centers.
4. **India to GCC Jobs Guide** - angle: a realistic step-by-step for Indian professionals. Strongest section: Credible Application Channels + relocation wording. Referrers: Indian overseas-career resources, alumni associations, engineering communities.
