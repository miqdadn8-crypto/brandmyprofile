## 7. Reviews strategy
**Allowed:** ask genuine customers after the service is complete; use a simple review link (from the GBP dashboard); neutral wording; reply to every genuine review.
**Not allowed:** incentives or discounts, fake or employee reviews, review gating (asking only happy customers), scripted wording, asking for "5 stars".

**WhatsApp request:**
> Hi [first name], thank you for working with BrandMyProfile. If you have a moment, we'd really value your honest feedback on Google: [review link]. Thank you!

**Email request:**
> Subject: Your feedback on BrandMyProfile
> Hi [first name], thanks for choosing BrandMyProfile for your [service]. If you're happy to share your experience, you can leave an honest review here: [review link]. It helps other professionals understand what to expect. Thank you for your time. - [Your name]

**Reply templates (adapt each; never keyword-stuff):**
- Positive: "Thank you, [name]. We're glad the process was useful, and we wish you the best with your applications."
- Detailed positive: "Thank you for the detailed feedback, [name]. It's helpful to hear that [specific point they mentioned]. Good luck with [their goal]."
- Neutral: "Thanks for your feedback, [name]. We'd like to hear what would have made it better; please message us on WhatsApp and we'll look into it."
- Negative: "We're sorry this wasn't the experience you expected, [name]. We'd like to understand what happened and put it right if we can; please contact us at +965 41103254 so we can look into it." (Be factual, never argue publicly, never disclose their details.)

**Never** write or publish invented reviews. Republish review text elsewhere only with the reviewer's permission and as written.
