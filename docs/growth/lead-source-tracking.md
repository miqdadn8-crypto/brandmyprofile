# Lead Source Tracking and Handling

No analytics is active (see [../analytics-readiness.md](../analytics-readiness.md)), so lead sources are logged **manually** in [lead-tracker.csv](./lead-tracker.csv). Ask "How did you find us?" naturally in the first WhatsApp exchange.

## Source values
`organic_google` | `google_business_profile` | `linkedin` | `instagram` | `whatsapp_direct` | `referral` | `paid_search` | `other`

Collect only: date, source, landing page, service interest, country, channel, qualified (y/n), converted (y/n), package, short note. **Never** store CV content, passport/ID details, full profiles or other sensitive data in the tracker.

## Qualified lead
Qualified = asks about a real service, has a target role or market, and is considering paid support.
Not qualified: spam; someone applying for a job at BrandMyProfile; someone asking whether a sample job is available; unrelated recruitment enquiries.

## WhatsApp response flow (reply quickly during real availability; do not promise 24/7 or fixed response times unless true)
1. Acknowledge.
2. Ask the target role.
3. Ask the target country.
4. Ask for the CV only if needed.
5. Recommend a service.
6. Share the relevant package.
7. Follow up **once** if there is no reply.

**First reply template:**
> Hi, thank you for contacting BrandMyProfile. I can help you review your CV and identify what can be improved. Which role and country are you currently targeting?

## Free CV review (what it is)
Scope: structure, clarity, ATS readability, positioning, relevance to the target role, obvious formatting issues. It is **not** a full rewrite and it does **not** give an ATS score (no such tool exists on the site). Keep it to a short written summary.

**Review reply template (WhatsApp):**
> **What is working:** [1-2 genuine strengths]
> **Main improvements:** [2-3 specific points]
> **Priority recommendation:** [the single most important change]
> **Suggested service:** [CV Writing / ATS CV / GCC CV / LinkedIn, one line why]
> **Next step:** [what to send or confirm; share the package link if interested]

Do not guarantee outcomes. Do not request sensitive documents beyond the CV.
