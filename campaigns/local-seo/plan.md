### The three costliest gaps
1. **Google files you under the wrong service.** Your category says career guidance, while the searches that bring paying clients say CV. Google matched "cv writing" to a "Resume service" listing that has fewer, older reviews than yours. *Evidence: map check, 17 Nov; Business Profile editor, 18 Nov.*
2. **Your website's text is hidden from many crawlers.** Every page sends its content inside a `<noscript>` block, and tools that read raw HTML saw only page titles. None of your pages turned up among ~180 sources behind 12 buyer questions, while Labeeb's dated, question-by-question pages did. *Evidence: site audit and AI-search audit, 17 Nov.*
3. **Your strongest proof never reaches the decision.** 4.9★ from 32 reviews doesn't appear on /kuwait, the page's reviews section shows only a link, and the price (from 4 KWD, the lowest human-written option found) sits below the first screen. *Evidence: phone screenshot and page teardown, 17 Nov.*

### Fix first
**Change the Google profile in one sitting:** set the primary category to *Resume service* (keep *Career guidance service* as additional), hide the address, and set the service area to all of Kuwait.
- **Owner time:** 15 minutes. Expect Google may ask you to re-verify, which is why all three changes happen together.
- **How we'll know:** in 2 weeks (week of 7 Dec), search "cv writing" and "resume writing" on Maps from the same spot as the 17 Nov check, and screenshot the list view. Success is BrandMyProfile appearing in that list.
- **Expectation:** distance from the searcher still counts on Maps, so you won't appear first everywhere in Kuwait. The website and AI answers carry you where the map doesn't.
