## 2. What to fix, ranked

1. **Change the primary category to "Resume service" (5 minutes).** In Business Profile → Edit profile → Business category, set the primary category to *Resume service* and keep *Career guidance service* as an additional category. Add *Career counselor* too if Google offers it. Your best-selling product is CV writing, and Google's category is a strong relevance signal on Maps for exactly the "cv writing" and "resume writing" searches that just showed a rival. Re-check the same phone search 1–2 weeks after the change.
2. **Remove your own review (2 minutes).** Your Reviews tab shows "Edit your review", so the signed-in account has reviewed BrandMyProfile. Google counts reviews from owners, staff and family as a conflict of interest, and profiles have been restricted for this, which can hide all reviews for a while. Delete it via **Edit your review → Delete**, and check that no staff or family member has reviewed either.
3. **Replace the "Thank you <name>" replies with real ones.** 89% of buyers expect owners to respond, and a row of identical one-line thank-yous looks automated. Section 3 has rewrites for the two newest; send me the other 30 and I'll do the rest.
4. **Keep the pace with an ask after every delivery** (section 5). You already have 32 reviews and some arriving this month. The goal now is to never go two weeks without a new one, because 32% of buyers look for reviews from the last two weeks.
5. **Check the profile facts:** "Open 24 hours" (keep it only if WhatsApp is genuinely answered at any hour, otherwise set real hours), service-area countries, and a few real photos (the team, a redacted before/after CV page). The full profile check is `/google-business-profile`.

## 5. The ask

**Who:** every client whose CV, LinkedIn or coaching work is finished, whatever their mood. Don't pick happy clients and don't run a "rate us first" survey.

**When:** at final delivery (files sent and last revision signed off), in the same WhatsApp chat. One reminder 3–5 days later, then stop. If a revision is still open, finish it first.

**Channel (updated 2026-11-19):** the review link is now Trustpilot (online-only business, Google profile at risk). WhatsApp in the open chat. A message sent while the client is still active in the chat (inside WhatsApp's 24-hour window) needs no template. Use email for clients who only used the form. The link comes from Business Profile → "Ask for reviews".

**Scripts** (put in the writer's real name):

At delivery:
> Hi Ahmed, your final CV is attached in Word and PDF. Thanks for working with me on it. If you have a minute, a Google review helps a small team like ours more than anything: [link]
> — Sara, BrandMyProfile

Reminder, day 3–5:
> Hi Ahmed, Sara from BrandMyProfile again. If you didn't get a chance, here's the Google review link: [link]. And if anything in the CV needs changing for a specific job, reply here and I'll sort it.

Email:
> Subject: Your final CV from BrandMyProfile
> Hi Ahmed, your final CV is attached. Thanks for working with us. If you have a minute, a short Google review helps other job seekers find us: [link]. Anything you'd like changed, reply to this email. Sara

Past clients from the last 6–12 months (ask once, a few a day over 3–4 weeks):
> Hi Priya, it's Sara at BrandMyProfile. We worked on your CV for the Dubai finance roles back in the spring. We're asking past clients for Google reviews this month. If you have a minute: [link]

**Never send:**
- "5 stars please"
- "Mention our fast reply / 4 KWD price"
- Any discount or freebie in return for a review
- Asks to staff, family or partner agencies
- A "Happy? Review us. Not happy? Message us first" gate

**Setup:**
1. Copy the review link from "Ask for reviews".
2. Save the two texts as WhatsApp Business quick replies (/review, /review2).
3. Add "Files sent → /review" to the delivery checklist and set a day-4 reminder for /review2.
4. Keep a monthly tally: CVs delivered, asks sent, reviews received, replies posted.

**Monthly target:** at least 4 new reviews a month (one every ~week). The visible rival adds roughly none, so the target is set by what buyers read (reviews from the last two weeks), not by catching up. At a cautious 25% of asked clients leaving a review, that means about 16 asks, roughly 16 finished CVs a month. Measure the real rate in December and redo the sum.
