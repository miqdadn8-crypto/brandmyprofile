## AI SEARCH AUDIT: BrandMyProfile
2026-11-17 · how AI assistants answer CV-writing questions from job seekers in Kuwait, the UAE, the GCC and India

### The read
**What I found:** When a job seeker asks for the best CV writer in Kuwait or Dubai, what a CV writer costs in Kuwait, or why their CV gets no interview calls, the sources the answers draw on name Labeeb in 9 of 10 observed answers and BrandMyProfile in none of 12. When someone asks "what is BrandMyProfile?", the answer describes a different company: BrandYourself, a US reputation-management firm.
**Why it matters:** The buyer who asks "how much does a CV writer cost in Kuwait?" is told "KWD 25 to 150", taken almost entirely from Labeeb's pages. Your Kuwait CV starts at 4 KWD. The cheapest human-written option in the market is missing from the one answer where price is the question.
**Why now:** Every money question showed the same gap, and the cost question hands a price-shopping buyer straight to a named competitor. The pages that should answer these questions already exist on your site, are well built and are readable by AI crawlers. They just aren't being found.
**What I recommend:** Confirm in Google Search Console that the six money pages are indexed, and fix whatever stops them. Then put "BrandMyProfile" on the profiles the engines already trust, so the name stops resolving to BrandYourself. We'll know it worked when a search for "BrandMyProfile" returns your site and the Kuwait cost question cites /kuwait/pricing at least once in a monthly re-run.

### Findings (typed, costliest first)
1. **[wrong or merged facts] "what is BrandMyProfile"**: the engines merge your name with BrandYourself. Until the name points to your site, every fix below compounds slowly, because engines can't confidently attribute your pages to an entity.
2. **[missing from results, though pages exist] Kuwait cost, best CV writer in Kuwait, ATS Kuwait, GCC format**: you have a strong candidate page for each one. /kuwait/pricing opens with a direct 4/7/10 KWD answer, and /blog/best-cv-writing-service-kuwait has a 980-word guide with a Quick Answer block. Both serve their text in the HTML to OAI-SearchBot and Googlebot, but only inside a `<noscript>` block that many text extractors discard (corrected after the same-day site audit; it is now its fix-first item). Both are in the sitemap, and robots.txt allows every crawler. Not one of them appeared among about 180 retrieved sources. This is a **found** problem, not a format problem: the pages are either not indexed or ranking too low to be retrieved. A quick `site:` check turned up none of your pages, but this search tool may not support that operator, so confirm in Search Console.
3. **[outside-source gap] best CV writer in Dubai / India → Gulf / LinkedIn UAE**: these answers lean on self-published "best of" lists (Google Sites, Yolasite, Blogspot), paid Indian media placements, review profiles (Trustpilot, Fiverr, Upwork) and directories. You're on none of them. Editing your own pages won't close this.
4. **[volume and freshness gap] the problem questions**: Labeeb wins with one dated 2026 page per buyer question, with the question in the URL, mirrored on Blogspot. Your equivalents (gcc-cv-mistakes, gcc-cv-format-guide, ats-cv-guide-kuwait) answer the same questions, so the gap is that they aren't found, not the content. Copying Labeeb's page count isn't the fix.
5. **[unmeasured]** Live ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overview answers were not captured. Every row above is web-search retrieval, one run per question. Your Google rating and reviews were not read.

### Where the answers come from
- **Category:** self-published listicles and directory or review profiles. The fix lane is citation earning, starting with real reviews on Google and a third-party review profile.
- **Comparison and cost:** competitor brand pages, mostly Labeeb's. Your own pages can win here once they're found, because you hold the fact the answer lacks (the 4 KWD price).
- **Problem:** country CV guides (VisualCV, StylingCV), job portals (Bayt, NaukriGulf) and Labeeb. Your existing guides can compete once indexed.
- **Brand:** reference and software-directory pages about a similarly named company. The fix lane is entity work.

### Fix plan
1. **Confirm and fix indexing (the prerequisite).** In Search Console, inspect /kuwait/pricing, /blog/best-cv-writing-service-kuwait, /blog/ats-cv-guide-kuwait, /blog/gcc-cv-format-guide, /blog/gcc-cv-mistakes and /blog/best-cv-writing-service-uae, request indexing for any that aren't indexed, and resubmit the sitemap. Then check the Generative AI performance report for impressions. *Addresses:* Q1, Q2, Q7–Q10. *Acceptance:* all six show "URL is on Google", and a search for "BrandMyProfile" returns brandmyprofile.com.
2. **Separate the name from BrandYourself.** Make the Organization schema's `sameAs` list every official profile (Google Business Profile, LinkedIn company page, Instagram, Facebook), and use the full phrase "BrandMyProfile, an online CV writing service based in Kuwait" in each profile's bio. Add a Bing Places listing, and a Trustpilot profile if you're willing to invite real customers. *Addresses:* Q11, Q12. *Acceptance:* "what is BrandMyProfile" describes your service in a re-run.
3. **Publish the price as a citable fact.** Add a one-paragraph, dated block to /kuwait/pricing and the Kuwait guide: "As of November 2026, a human-written CV for Kuwait jobs costs 4 KWD (Essential) to 10 KWD (Executive) at BrandMyProfile, with a free CV review before you pay." Keep the date current each quarter. *Addresses:* Q10, Q5. *Acceptance:* the Kuwait cost question names BrandMyProfile or cites /kuwait/pricing.
4. **Get onto the sources that answer category questions.** Collect real Google reviews (the /reviews skill), then pitch the recruiter-compiled and Gulf career lists the answers actually cite, using real proof only. Skip pay-to-play badges, or label them as paid if you buy one. *Addresses:* Q1–Q4. *Acceptance:* named in at least one category answer.
5. **Hold off on new content.** Your question-matched pages already exist. Writing more before the first ones are found adds pages nobody retrieves.
