## AI SEARCH FIXES: BrandMyProfile
2026-11-17 · fixes from the 2026-11-17 audit, in leverage order

Most of the standard on-site work was already in place before this run: Organization JSON-LD with `sameAs`, a hand-written llms.txt extended at build time with live prices and guides, an IndexNow script with its key file live, question-matched answer blocks ("What is BrandMyProfile?", "How much does CV writing cost in <country>?"), server-rendered HTML for every page, and robots.txt open to all crawlers. The audit's gap is therefore mostly off-site, and this file is mostly a work list for you, not code.

### 1. Get the money pages indexed (do this first, ~20 min, you)

The audit found none of your pages among about 180 retrieved sources, although every one serves full HTML to crawlers. Until Google and Bing index them, nothing below compounds.

**In Google Search Console** (property `https://www.brandmyprofile.com`):
1. Use URL Inspection on each page below. Note "URL is on Google" or the exact reason it isn't ("Discovered – currently not indexed", "Crawled – currently not indexed", "Duplicate, Google chose different canonical").
   - /kuwait/pricing
   - /blog/best-cv-writing-service-kuwait
   - /blog/ats-cv-guide-kuwait
   - /blog/gcc-cv-format-guide
   - /blog/gcc-cv-mistakes
   - /blog/best-cv-writing-service-uae
2. Click "Request indexing" on any that are not indexed (the limit is about 10 a day).
3. Under Sitemaps, resubmit `sitemap.xml` and confirm "Discovered URLs" reads 108.
4. Under Performance, open the Search results report, filter Query contains "brandmyprofile", and record impressions and position. This is your brand-search baseline.
5. If the **Generative AI** performance report is available on the property, record its numbers too.

**In Bing Webmaster Tools:** import the site from Search Console (one click), then run `npm run indexnow` after the next deploy.

**What the result means:**
- "Crawled – currently not indexed" across most pages means Google sees the site as low-authority or too similar across regions. The next job is /site-audit, plus earning mentions (sections 3 and 4).
- "Discovered – not indexed" means crawl budget or no internal links. Fix it with requests and links from the homepage.
- Indexed but not ranking means it's a rank problem. The answer is outside mentions (sections 3 and 4), not more pages.

**Done when:** all six show "URL is on Google", and a search for "BrandMyProfile" returns brandmyprofile.com first.

### 3. Separate the name from BrandYourself (entity work, ~1 hour, you)

The engines answer "what is BrandMyProfile" from Wikipedia, G2 and Entrepreneur pages about BrandYourself, because no third-party page about you exists. Your `sameAs` currently lists LinkedIn, Instagram and the Google Business Profile. The fix is more independent pages that say the same thing in the same words:

1. **Use the same descriptor on every profile:** "BrandMyProfile is an online CV writing and LinkedIn optimization service based in Kuwait, serving job seekers across the GCC and India. brandmyprofile.com". Paste it into the LinkedIn company "About", the Instagram bio and the Google Business Profile description.
2. **Add profiles that engines read**, then add each URL to `sameAs` in [src/lib/seo/organization.json](../../src/lib/seo/organization.json):
   - Bing Places for Business (feeds Bing and Copilot)
   - A Facebook page, if you don't have one
   - Trustpilot, but only if you'll invite every real customer, not a chosen few. An empty or cherry-picked profile does more harm than none.
3. **Don't buy a Wikipedia page or fake press.** Neither is available at your stage, and both backfire.

**Done when:** a re-run of "what is BrandMyProfile" describes your service.

### 4. Get onto the sources the category answers cite (citation earning)

The "best CV writer in Kuwait / Dubai / India for Gulf jobs" answers lean on lists and profiles, not on providers' own pages. Targets the audit actually saw cited:

| Source (seen cited) | Kind | What it would take |
|---|---|---|
| Image Consulting Institute "Best Resume Writers in Dubai / India" | directory | Find their listing or submission page and apply with real credentials. The contact is unknown; check the page. |
| "Compiled by Recruiters and Head Hunters from GCC" (sites.google.com) | self-published list | The author is anonymous. Low value, skip unless a contact surfaces. |
| Find My Profession "Best <country> Resume Writer" badges | pay-to-play | Paid placement. If you buy one, treat it as an ad, never as an award. |
| ThePrint "brandit" top-10 lists (India) | paid media placement | Sponsored content. The same caveat applies: label it as paid if you use it. |
| Fiverr / Upwork resume-writer categories | marketplace | A real seller profile under the BrandMyProfile name, with your actual price, earns reviews where answers already look. |
| Bayt, GulfTalent, NaukriGulf career articles | job-portal editorial | Pitch a guest article from real expertise (for example "the 5 GCC CV mistakes we see most in free reviews"), with a byline that names BrandMyProfile. |

There are no fake reviews, no invented awards, and no publishing your own "best CV writers" list. Answers cite third-party lists, and a self-ranking list reads as spam.

### 5. Citable assets: briefs for original data

Every cost and problem answer needs a number, and the only numbers in them now are Labeeb's prices and generic ATS claims. You hold data nobody else has: your free CV reviews. Two briefs, both buildable from records you already keep. **Publish only counts you actually tallied.**

**Asset A: "What we found in <N> free CV reviews for GCC jobs (2026)"**
- *Answerable stat:* "Of <N> CVs BrandMyProfile reviewed for GCC jobs in 2026, <X>% used a two-column or table layout that ATS software misreads." (Fill in from your review notes. Add 3–5 such stats.)
- *Query-adjacent:* answers "why am I not getting interview calls from Gulf companies" and "ATS-friendly CV Kuwait", where you're currently absent.
- *Repeatable:* re-tally every January for a new edition and date.
- *Page:* /seo-content, as one article with a dated method note (how many reviews, which months, what counted).

**Asset B: "What a CV writer costs in Kuwait and the GCC, 2026"**
- *Answerable stat:* the published package prices of 5–8 named GCC providers, each with its source link and the date checked, placed beside your own.
- *Query-adjacent:* answers "how much does a CV writing service cost in Kuwait", where the answer today quotes only Labeeb.
- *Repeatable:* re-check every quarter.
- *Caution:* this is collected data, so it's weaker than Asset A. Price only what each provider publishes, and keep it a factual table, not an attack.

### Skipped and why
- **Answer-block retrofits:** your candidate pages already open with direct, self-contained answers. The gap is that they aren't found, not that they can't be lifted.
- **More schema:** it's already present, and it isn't a citation lever.
- **New content pages:** you already have a page for every gap question. Writing more before these are indexed adds pages nobody retrieves.
- **Sitemap changes:** the 64 prerendered pages missing from it are job listings and utility pages (free review, schedule, success stories, privacy, terms), which looks intentional.
