# Brief: Kuwait CV Template Pack
- **Goal:** capture "kuwait cv format word/pdf/template" searchers and turn them into free CV reviews.
- **Gap vs competitors (searched 2026-11-19):** free Word templates everywhere are generic (Standout CV, ResumeLab, MyPerfectCV); the only GCC-specific template found is paid ($5, Selar); none are Kuwait-specific or role-specific from a CV writer.
- **Distribution:** link from /blog/kuwait-cv-format-guide (template section), /kuwait free review section, Google Business Profile post (week 1 or 3), Instagram/LinkedIn posts, WhatsApp greeting message ("want our free Kuwait CV template?").
- **Measure:** WhatsApp chats starting "I used your Kuwait CV template"; resource_download events once analytics is on.
