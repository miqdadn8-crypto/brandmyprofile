## SITE AUDIT: LinkedIn pages on brandmyprofile.com
Nov 18, 2026 · 5 LinkedIn service pages and 2 LinkedIn guides, for getting found on Google and in AI answers

**Pages in scope:**
- Service pages: /kuwait, /uae, /saudi, /qatar and /india, each at `/services/linkedin-makeover`
- Guides: /blog/linkedin-headline-gulf-jobs and /blog/optimize-linkedin-profile-visibility

**Fix first: crawlers that don't run JavaScript see less than a fifth of each LinkedIn service page, and it sits inside `<noscript>`.** After JavaScript runs, /kuwait/services/linkedin-makeover has about 770 words: before/after examples, "Every Section Has a Job to Do", how it works, three priced levels (5 / 7 / 10 KWD) and FAQs. The HTML the server sends holds about 140 words, all inside the `<noscript>` block from yesterday's audit: a short summary, the generic "BrandMyProfile is an online career-support service…" line and links. The same holds on all five region pages (125–142 words sent vs ~770 shown). Google sees the full page after rendering; AI crawlers and other tools that read raw HTML see a thin page or nothing. **Fix:** the same one change from yesterday's audit (render the page content inside `#root`, not `<noscript>`). Until then, make sure the static version carries the real substance (what's rewritten, the three levels with prices, and the FAQ answers), not only a summary. Developer time: covered by the site-wide fix. Extending the static version for these 5 pages alone is about 1 hour.

**Then, in this order:**

1. **The visible headline never says "LinkedIn profile writing" or the country.** Every region page shows the same H1: *"Build a LinkedIn Profile Recruiters Understand"*. The service and place appear only in the browser title and lower headings. Searchers type "linkedin profile writing kuwait" or "linkedin profile optimization service", and the H1 is one of the strongest signals of what a page is about. **Fix:** make the H1 say what and where, and keep the current line as the subheading. For example, **"LinkedIn Profile Writing in Kuwait"** with *"Build a LinkedIn profile recruiters understand"* underneath (and the matching country on each region page). The static (no-JavaScript) H1 already says "LinkedIn Profile Writing & Optimization in Kuwait", so the two versions disagree today. Align them. About 20 minutes.
2. **The five region pages are 95–96% the same text.** Only the country name in one heading and the prices change (Kuwait vs UAE: 95% identical; Kuwait vs India: 96%). Your hreflang tags correctly tell Google these are regional versions, which prevents a duplicate-content penalty. But Google will still mostly show one of them, and none of them gives a reason to rank in its own country. **Fix:** add one genuinely local section per page, 100–200 words. Examples: what Kuwait recruiters and Kuwaitization roles look for on LinkedIn, how UAE recruiters search, and the India-to-GCC angle on the India page. Start with Kuwait, your home market. About 1 hour per page.
3. **Your best LinkedIn guide has no links pointing to it.** /blog/linkedin-headline-gulf-jobs (about 1,300 words, specific to Gulf job seekers) is in the sitemap, but none of the 102 pages crawled yesterday links to it, including the LinkedIn service pages. **Fix:** link it from all five LinkedIn service pages ("How to write a LinkedIn headline for Gulf jobs") and from the career guides hub. 15 minutes.
4. **The other LinkedIn article is thin and generic.** /blog/optimize-linkedin-profile-visibility has about 230 words of general advice ("the Middle East job market") and one link pointing to it. Next to the headline guide it adds little, and thin articles weigh down a small site. **Fix:** either expand it into a real guide with specific steps and Gulf examples, or merge it into the headline guide with a 301 redirect. Merging is faster (30 minutes).
5. **Page names don't match.** The Kuwait title says "LinkedIn Profile Writing & Optimization in Kuwait | BrandMyProfile" (66 characters, so Google cuts it off), while the other four say "LinkedIn Profile Makeover in <country>". "Makeover" is your product name, but buyers more likely search "LinkedIn profile writing" or "optimization". Confirm with real search data before changing four pages. **Fix for now:** shorten the Kuwait title to "LinkedIn Profile Writing in Kuwait | BrandMyProfile" (51 characters). Run `/keyword-research` on LinkedIn terms before renaming the rest. 5 minutes.

**Also worth doing:**
- Link each LinkedIn page to the matching CV page and back (the Professional CV package already includes a full LinkedIn makeover, so say so on the LinkedIn page).
- Add the real LinkedIn prices to Google Business Profile services (from 5 KWD).

**Checked and fine:** all 5 service pages and both guides return 200, are indexable, have one H1, a self-referencing canonical and hreflang between regions, and are in the sitemap. The service pages sit 2 clicks from the homepage with 16–19 internal links each. Prices show on the rendered pages (Kuwait 5 / 7 / 10 KWD, UAE 72 / 101 / 144 AED, India ₹699 / ₹999 / ₹1,699).
