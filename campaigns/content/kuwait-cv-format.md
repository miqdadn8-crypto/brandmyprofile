# Kuwait CV Format (2026): Layout, Free Template and Examples

*Updated November 2026*

There's no official Kuwait CV format. Kuwait employers expect a clean, reverse-chronological CV in English (Arabic for some local and public-sector roles): a short professional summary, your skills, work experience written as results, then education and certifications, usually on two pages or fewer. Leave your Civil ID and passport number off it. Below is the layout we use, a template you can copy, and what changes for nurses, freshers and drivers.

## Is there an official Kuwait CV format?

No. No ministry or employer body publishes a mandatory CV format for Kuwait, and a template sold as "the official Kuwait CV" is just a template. What Kuwait employers share is a set of expectations: a clear structure, recent experience first, achievements rather than duties, and only the personal details that matter for the job. Some employers and application portals add their own requirements, and when they do, theirs win.

We'd rather you get that from us than discover it after paying for a "Kuwait format" download.

## The Kuwait CV layout, section by section

A Kuwait CV works best in this order: contact details and target role, a three-to-four-line professional summary, key skills, work experience in reverse-chronological order, education, certifications and licences, then languages. One column, standard headings, no tables or text boxes. That order lets a recruiter see who you are and what you're applying for in the first few seconds, and it's the order applicant tracking software reads most reliably.

What goes in each part:

- **Contact details:** full name, phone with country code (+965 if you're in Kuwait), email, LinkedIn URL, and your city. Your city and country, not your street address.
- **Target role line:** the job title you're applying for, written the way the advert writes it.
- **Professional summary:** three or four lines on what you do, for whom, and the one or two results you're proudest of. Rewrite it for each application. A summary that could open any CV in any country does nothing.
- **Key skills:** 8 to 12 skills taken from the job description, tools named exactly ("SAP MM", not "ERP systems").
- **Work experience:** job title, employer, city, month and year. Then three to six bullets per role, each one a result: what you did, with what, and what changed.
- **Education:** degree, institution, country, year.
- **Certifications and licences:** name, issuing body, year and expiry where it applies. For licensed jobs, this section matters as much as experience.
- **Languages:** English, Arabic and any others, with an honest level.

## Personal details: what to include, what to leave off

Include what helps an employer judge whether you can start: nationality, current location, visa or residency status, and notice period if you're employed. Leave off your Civil ID number, passport number, full home address, religion and marital status. Employers don't need these to shortlist you, and a CV gets forwarded far more widely than you'd expect.

Date of birth is optional. Some Kuwait employers still ask for it; plenty don't, and leaving it off has never cost a good candidate an interview.

## Photo or no photo?

Leave the photo off unless the advert asks for one or the role is customer-facing and the employer is clearly local. You'll still see photos on many CVs in Kuwait, but a photo adds nothing for applicant tracking software, and multinational employers increasingly prefer CVs without one. If you include one, make it a plain, recent, professional headshot. Never a cropped holiday picture.

## How long should a Kuwait CV be?

One page for freshers and candidates with under three years' experience, two pages for most experienced professionals. Go longer only for senior, technical or project-heavy careers where the projects themselves are the evidence, such as engineering and oil and gas. The test isn't page count. It's whether every line would make a recruiter more likely to call you. If a line wouldn't, cut it.

## Word or PDF?

Send a PDF unless the advert or recruiter asks for Word. A PDF keeps your layout identical on every screen. Keep a Word version too, because some recruiters and job portals ask for an editable file, and you'll want it for tailoring each application. Name the file properly: "Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf", not "CV final new 2.pdf".

## Making it work with applicant tracking systems

Larger employers in Kuwait, especially banks, oil and gas companies, telecoms and multinationals, often screen applications through an applicant tracking system before a person reads them. These systems read plain text best. Use a single column, standard headings ("Work Experience", not "My Journey"), no tables, text boxes, icons or skill bars, and put the job description's key terms in your summary and bullets as plain words. Our [ATS CV guide for Kuwait](/blog/ats-cv-guide-kuwait) goes deeper on this.

## Kuwait CV format by role

### Nurses and Ministry of Health applications

For nursing and other healthcare roles, put your licence and registration details near the top, directly under the summary. Include your licence or registration number if the employer asks for it, the issuing body, and the expiry. List your clinical areas (ICU, ER, OR, paediatrics), the bed capacity or patient load you handled, and the systems you've used. Keep continuing education and certifications such as BLS or ACLS in their own section, with dates.

Health licensing in Kuwait involves its own document and verification steps, separate from your CV. Check the current Ministry of Health requirements for your role before you apply, and keep your CV consistent with the documents you'll submit.

### Freshers and graduates

If you have little or no work experience, put education first: degree, university, year, and your grade if it's strong. Follow it with internships, training, university projects and part-time work, each written as what you did and what came of it ("Built the inventory tracker our student society still uses", not "Participated in society activities"). One page. A clear target role at the top matters even more for freshers, because you're asking the reader to imagine you in a job you haven't done yet.

### Drivers

Lead with your licence: the type, the vehicle classes you're licensed for, and the expiry. Then experience, written specifically: vehicle types driven, delivery or route areas in Kuwait, and how long you've driven professionally. If your safety record is clean, say so plainly. Mention any company-specific training such as defensive driving or hazardous goods with its date. Keep it to one page.

## Common mistakes on Kuwait CVs

The mistake we correct most is duty-writing: "Responsible for stock and reports" instead of "Kept stock records in Excel and cut month-end reconciliation from three days to one". (That example is illustrative. Your real numbers belong in your real CV.) Close behind it are summaries copied from a template, personal details nobody asked for, mixed date formats, and one CV sent unchanged to every advert. None of these get you rejected on their own. Together, they're why a CV gets a ten-second skim instead of a phone call.

## Kuwait CV checklist

- Target role at the top, matching the advert's wording
- Summary rewritten for this application
- Experience in reverse-chronological order, written as results
- Key terms from the job description in plain text
- Single column, standard headings, no tables or graphics
- No Civil ID, passport number or full address
- Licences and certifications with expiry dates where relevant
- PDF sent, Word version kept
- File named with your name

## FAQ

### Should I write my CV in Arabic or English for Kuwait?
English works for most private-sector and multinational employers. An Arabic version helps for government, semi-government and locally owned employers that work mainly in Arabic, or when the advert itself is in Arabic.

### Do I need a cover letter for jobs in Kuwait?
Not always, but it helps when you apply directly to an employer rather than through a recruiter, or when you're changing country or career. Keep it to three short paragraphs. See our guide to [cover letters for GCC jobs](/blog/cover-letter-gcc-jobs).

### Is a Kuwait CV different from a UAE or Saudi CV?
Mostly no. The structure is the same across the GCC; the differences are in personal-detail norms and which employers expect Arabic. Our [GCC CV format guide](/blog/gcc-cv-format-guide) compares them.

### Can I use the same CV for every job in Kuwait?
Keep one core CV, but change the target role, summary and key skills for each application. It takes ten minutes per application and makes a bigger difference than any formatting choice.
