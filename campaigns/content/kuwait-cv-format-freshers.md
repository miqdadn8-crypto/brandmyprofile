# Kuwait CV Format for Freshers: A One-Page Template That Works

*Updated November 2026*

A fresher CV for Kuwait should be one page, with your education first, then internships, projects and part-time work written as results, then skills and languages. Put a clear target role at the top. Leave off your Civil ID and passport number. With little work history, the order of the page does more of the persuading than any single line on it.

## What does a fresher CV for Kuwait look like?

A fresher CV for Kuwait is a single page in this order: contact details and target role, a two-to-three-line summary, education, internships and projects, skills, training and certifications, languages, and one line of activities. It flips the usual CV, where experience leads, because education is your strongest evidence. Job seekers in the region agree: in a [2018 Bayt.com poll](https://kuwaittimes.com/bayt-com-provides-insights-perfect-cv-look-like-fresh-graduate) of 9,144 job seekers across the Middle East, including Kuwait, 56.5 percent named education the most important part of a fresh graduate's CV, well ahead of work experience (19.5 percent) and skills (19.4 percent).

## Why education goes first

Education goes first because it's the only section where a fresher has as much to show as anyone else applying. Put the degree and major, the university, the country and the year, and your grade if it helps you. Then add one or two lines that prove you did something with the degree: a final-year project, a course directly relevant to the job, a competition, an award.

"BSc Accounting, 2026" tells a recruiter you finished. "Final-year project: built a cash-flow forecast for a small trading company in Excel" tells them you can do the job.

## How to write experience when you don't have much

Write every internship, project, part-time job and volunteer role as what you did and what came of it, not as what you "participated in". A three-month internship described as "prepared bank reconciliations for two client audits" beats a year described as "assisted the finance team".

Count everything real:
- Internships, including short or unpaid ones
- University projects, especially ones with a client, a team or a result
- Part-time and weekend jobs, even outside your field, because they show you turn up and handle people
- Volunteering and society roles where you were responsible for something
- Family business work, if you genuinely did it

The same 2018 poll found 83 percent thought freshers should include any work experience "even if it isn't very relevant", and 89 percent called internships and volunteer work essential. We'd add one condition: write the irrelevant job in one line, and give the lines to the experience that matches the advert.

## The one-page rule

Keep a fresher CV to one page. It's the format employers expect from a graduate (79.5 percent in that poll said one page at most), and it forces you to cut the filler that weakens graduate CVs: school-level qualifications once you have a degree, hobbies with no evidence behind them, and a "references available on request" line.

If you can't fit everything, cut the least relevant item, not the font size. Ten-point text squeezed to the margins tells a recruiter you couldn't decide what mattered.

## Your summary and target role

Put the job title you're applying for at the top, written the way the advert writes it, then a two-to-three-line summary: your degree, the role you want, and the strongest single thing you've done. Rewrite both for every application. "Accounting graduate seeking a graduate accountant role; completed a three-month audit internship and built a cash-flow model for a final-year project" is specific enough to be believed.

Skip "hard-working, motivated team player". Every fresher writes it, so it tells the reader nothing.

## Personal details for freshers in Kuwait

Include your nationality, your current location, your residency status if you're an expat, and when you can start. For many graduates who grew up in Kuwait on a family residency, saying so up front answers the recruiter's first question: can this person work here, and how soon?

Leave off your Civil ID number, passport number, full home address, religion and marital status. Add a photo only if the advert asks for one or the employer is clearly local and expects it. It adds nothing for applicant tracking software, though many graduates in the region still include one (three in four respondents in the 2018 poll thought a photo should be added).

## If you're a Kuwaiti national

Kuwaiti graduates applying for government jobs register through the Civil Service Commission's system, which has its own process and forms. Your CV is for private-sector employers, banks, oil and gas companies, and direct applications. For those, an English CV is standard, and an Arabic version is worth having for employers that work mainly in Arabic.

## Skills, languages and training

List 6 to 10 skills taken from the job description, tools named exactly ("Excel: pivot tables, XLOOKUP", "QuickBooks", "Python"), not adjectives. Give your languages with an honest level. Arabic and English together are a real advantage in Kuwait, so say how well you use each. List short courses and certificates with the provider and year. A completed online course in the tool the advert names is worth a line; a list of ten half-finished ones isn't.

## Common fresher CV mistakes

The mistakes we see most on graduate CVs are an objective that could belong to anyone, a duty list instead of results ("responsible for", "participated in"), two pages padded with school grades and hobbies, a template with columns and icons that tracking software misreads, and one CV sent unchanged to every advert. None of them is fatal on its own. Together they're why a graduate CV gets skimmed in seconds.

Here's the difference one line makes (an illustrative example, not a real client):

- Before: "Participated in a group project for marketing class."
- After: "Led a 4-person team that surveyed 120 shoppers in Salmiya and presented a pricing recommendation to the course panel."

The second version has a role, a number, a place and an outcome. That's what a recruiter can picture you doing again.

## FAQ

### How long should a fresher CV be for Kuwait?
One page. A fresh graduate rarely has enough relevant material to fill two, and a recruiter skimming dozens of graduate CVs reads the first page only.

### Should I put my GPA on my CV?
Include it if it's strong (roughly 3.0 out of 4.0 or above, or a merit or distinction). Leave it out if it isn't; nobody is required to list a grade, and a weak one draws attention away from your projects and internships.

### Do freshers need a cover letter in Kuwait?
Send one whenever you apply directly to a company. With little track record, three short paragraphs on why this employer, what you'd bring and when you can start do work the CV can't. Our guide to [cover letters for GCC jobs](/blog/cover-letter-gcc-jobs) has the structure.

### Is the format different for jobs in Dubai or Saudi Arabia?
Barely. The one-page, education-first layout works across the GCC; the main differences are in personal-detail norms and which employers expect Arabic. See the [GCC CV format guide](/blog/gcc-cv-format-guide).
