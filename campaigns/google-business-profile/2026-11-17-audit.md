### Risks found

1. **The address may be shown for a business with no office.** Your website says on every page that "Services are delivered online; there is no walk-in office", and the code notes a *service-area* profile was intended. Yet Maps shows a pin at a Kuwait City 53700 address. Google's rule is that a business that doesn't receive customers at its address must hide it and list service areas instead. A visible address with no customer-facing office is a common reason for suspension, especially once a competitor or Google's checks notice it. **Check:** Edit profile → Location → Business location. If customers can't walk in there during business hours, switch the address to *hidden* and add service areas in Kuwait (Kuwait City, Hawalli, Salmiya, Farwaniya, Ahmadi, Jahra, up to 20). Service areas must stay within about 2 hours' drive, so other GCC countries can't be service areas. Keep those on the website.
2. **"Open 24 hours" must be true.** If WhatsApp isn't answered overnight, a customer who messages at 2am and hears nothing until noon has been told something false. Set your real response hours. Google accepts plain-language hours.
3. **Your own review is on the profile** (the signed-in account shows "Edit your review"). It's a conflict of interest under Google's review policy, so delete it. This is covered in the reviews plan.
4. **Not observed, owner to check in the editor:**
